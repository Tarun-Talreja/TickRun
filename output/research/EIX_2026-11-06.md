## 1. What they actually do (3 sentences max)
Edison International is the holding company for Southern California Edison (SCE), a regulated electric utility serving approximately 15 million customers across 50,000 square miles of central, coastal, and Southern California. It generates, transmits, and distributes electricity under a cost-of-service regulatory model where rates are set by the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) to recover approved costs plus a return on equity. Revenue comes almost entirely from regulated rate-base investments in grid infrastructure, wildfire mitigation, and clean energy programs.
