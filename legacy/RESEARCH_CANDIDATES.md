## Methodology & Scoring Framework

### Composite Score Formula
```
Score = 0.2×Quality + 0.2×Valuation + 0.15×Profitability 
       + 0.1×Risk + 0.15×Recurring + 0.2×AI_Exposure
```

### Hard Gates (Survival Required)
- Market cap: $300M–$10B
- Current ratio ≥ 1.0
- Debt/EBITDA ≤ 5.0
- Short % of float ≤ 20%
- Positive free cash flow

### Factor Definitions
| Factor | Meaning | Good Score |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| **Quality** | Financial strength, margins, ROIC, FCF | 80+ |
| **Valuation** | P/E, EV/EBITDA, FCF yield relative to growth | 60+ |
| **Profitability** | Operating margin, gross margin, net margin | 85+ |
| **Risk** | Balance sheet, debt, competitive moat, volatility | 70+ |
| **Recurring** | Subscription/contracted revenue (industry heuristic) | 45+ |
| **AI Exposure** | Direct/indirect beneficiary of AI/DC/infrastructure | 20+ is signal |

### Data Quality Notes
- **ROIC proxy:** yfinance does not provide true ROIC; using ROE as proxy (overstates ROIC for leveraged companies)
- **Recurring revenue:** Sub-industry whitelist heuristic, NOT actual % of revenue from subscriptions/contracts
- **EV/EBITDA:** Some financials missing (insurance/asset management do not disclose EBITDA; using alternative metrics)
- **Forward P/E:** Based on analyst estimates; accuracy varies
